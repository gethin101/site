About Me

Hello, I’m Gethin — a person who likes building things, breaking things, fixing things, and learning how they work. I’ve always had a habit of diving into projects head-first, whether they involve electronics, mechanics, software, or something completely random.

I’m based in the UK, and most of my projects happen in a garage that leaks when it rains. My interests shift with the seasons, but as a rule of thumb: if it has wheels, a motor, or electricity running through it, I’m interested.

This website started as a place to document my projects — partly so I don’t forget how I built things, and partly because I enjoy sharing the process. It’s also a convenient way to host resources, notes, and experiments online.

About This Website

The site is intentionally simple. No adverts, no cookies, no AI-generated content — just a clean layout and a collection of things I’ve made or learned. It’s always changing, and sometimes things break, but that’s part of the charm.

If you find any issues, feel free to reach out. Otherwise, enjoy exploring the projects and the chaos behind them.
